=== PAGE 2 ===
Introduction
The efficiency of the laboratory at Consulting Engineering and Contractors is hindered by the absence of a
structured daily work planning system. While the project office effectively uses a whiteboard to manage
daily tasks, replicating this in the laboratory is impractical due to costs and scalability challenges. To
address this, a modern Trello-based work management system is proposed.
Figure :Trello login page in windows
Trello is a user-friendly, cost-effective application that integrates seamlessly into workflows, making it
ideal for laboratory operations. This system will streamline task assignments, enhance communication, and
foster accountability, leveraging the technical expertise of trainers, material engineers, and technicians. By
adopting Trello, the laboratory can address inefficiencies, improve productivity, and create a collaborative
work environment. This proposal details a practical and scalable implementation plan to make Trello the
foundation of daily management in the laboratory.

=== PAGE 3 ===
Problem Statement
The laboratory currently lacks a formal system for communicating daily tasks and targets. Unlike the
project office, which utilizes a whiteboard for planning, laboratory employees do not have a shared
understanding of daily objectives. This results in:
● Poor utilization of time and resources.
● Misalignment of efforts with organizational goals.
● Decreased employee morale due to uncertainty.
● Potential redundancy or overlap in tasks.
This managerial gap has a direct impact on laboratory productivity and project timelines, necessitating a
structured approach to daily work planning.

=== PAGE 4 ===
Objectives
This proposal aims to:
● Introduce a daily work planning and target-setting system in the laboratory.
● Ensure all laboratory employees understand their tasks and responsibilities.
● Foster better coordination and communication among laboratory team members.
● Improve overall efficiency and productivity in the laboratory.

=== PAGE 5 ===
Proposed Solution
To address this managerial challenge, the following solutions are proposed:
1. Trello for Trainers and Material Engineers:
o Utilize Trello, a user-friendly and free task management tool, to assign and track daily tasks for
trainers and material engineers in the laboratory. As technically proficient team members, they
can easily adopt and maintain this system.
2. Whiteboard for Lab Helpers and Technicians:
o Introduce a physical whiteboard in the laboratory to outline daily tasks and targets for lab helpers
and technicians. This approach mirrors the successful system implemented in the project office,
ensuring consistency and accessibility.
3. Daily Morning Meetings:
o Conduct a 10 – 15 minute meeting each morning in the laboratory to review the daily work plan,
set targets, and address any immediate concerns.
4. Progress Tracking:
o Implement a system to review the progress of tasks at the end of each day, identifying any delays
or areas for improvement.
5. Feedback Mechanism:
o Encourage laboratory employees to provide feedback on the work planning process, fostering
continuous improvement and adaptability.

Expected Benefits
● Increased clarity and focus for all laboratory team members.
● Enhanced coordination and collaboration within the laboratory.
● Improved utilization of time and resources.
● Alignment of daily tasks with broader organizational goals.
● Higher employee satisfaction and motivation.

Resources Required

=== PAGE 9 ===
Conclusion
Extending the structured daily work plan and target-setting system from the project office to the laboratory
is essential to address current inefficiencies. By leveraging Trello for trainers and material engineers and
introducing a whiteboard system for lab helpers and technicians, this proposal ensures clarity, better
coordination, and enhanced productivity in the laboratory. With clear implementation steps and minimal
resource requirements, this solution is practical, efficient, and impactful.
