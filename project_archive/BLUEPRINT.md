## 1. Executive Summary & Website Objectives

This website serves as a definitive, industrial-grade portfolio documenting the 24-week industrial engineering internship of **Premakumara H.P.S.** as a **Trainee Material Engineer** at **Consulting Engineering & Contractors (Pvt) Ltd (CEC)**.

The primary objective is to present an exhaustive, visually stunning, and technically rigorous showcase of engineering competence across:
1. **Civil & Geotechnical Materials Testing:** 13 standardized laboratory and in-situ field tests adhering to ASTM, BS, and ICTAD standards.
2. **Engineering Software Development & Process Automation:** Two standalone Excel VBA macro systems designed to automate Dynamic Cone Penetration (DCP) report generation (with RMSD layer boundary detection) and Request for Inspection (RFI) / QA management (529 BOQ items across 3 linked workbooks).
3. **Pavement Design & Highway Engineering:** RDA Pavement Design Manual Chart 2 implementation, Structural Number ($SN$) calculations, and asphalt concrete volumetric evaluation.
4. **Scientific Research & Innovation:** RDA-commissioned feasibility study on optimizing asphalt wearing course thickness (reducing from 40mm to 25mm/30mm on Road COL/2) to achieve major cost savings while maintaining structural longevity.
5. **Managerial Practices & Occupational Health & Safety (HSE):** Trello-based daily lab task scheduling system proposal, toolbox safety briefings, PPE enforcement, environmental mitigations, crack surveys, and organizational SWOT analysis.

## 4. Key Engineering Equations & Mathematical Formulations

1. **Dynamic Cone Penetration CBR Correlation:**
   $$CBR = 292 \times DPI^{-1.12}$$
   *(where $DPI = \text{mm/blow}$ penetration rate)*

2. **Root Mean Square Deviation (RMSD) for Soil Layer Breakpoints:**
   $$RMSD = \sqrt{\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N} (d_i - \hat{d}_i)^2}$$

3. **Compaction Dry Density ($\rho_d$) & Degree of Compaction ($C\%$):**
   $$\rho_d = \frac{\rho_{\text{wet}}}{1 + w}, \quad C\% = \left(\frac{\rho_{d,\text{field}}}{MDD}\right) \times 100\%$$

4. **Particle Size Distribution Coefficients:**
   $$C_u = \frac{d_{60}}{d_{10}}, \quad C_k = \frac{d_{30}^2}{d_{60} \times d_{10}}$$

5. **Plasticity Index:**
   $$PI = LL - PL$$

6. **Flexible Pavement Structural Number ($SN$):**
   $$SN = a_1 D_1 + a_2 D_2 + a_3 D_3 + \dots = (20 \times 0.20) + (175 \times 0.12) + (125 \times 0.095) = 36.875$$

7. **Concrete Compressive Strength ($f_{cu}$):**
   $$f_{cu} = \frac{P}{A} \quad (\text{MPa})$$

8. **Aggregate Impact Value (AIV) & Flakiness Index (FI):**
   $$AIV = \left(\frac{W_2}{W_1}\right) \times 100\%, \quad FI = \left(\frac{\sum W_{\text{flaky}}}{\sum W_{\text{total}}}\right) \times 100\%$$

9. **Ten Percent Fines Value (TFV):**
   $$TFV = \frac{14 f}{m + 4} \quad (\text{kN})$$

10. **Asphalt Air Voids ($V_a$) & Voids in Mineral Aggregate (VMA):**
    $$V_a = \left(1 - \frac{G_{mb}}{G_{mm}}\right) \times 100\%, \quad VMA = 100 - \frac{G_{mb} \times P_s}{G_{sb}}$$
